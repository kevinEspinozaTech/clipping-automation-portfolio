# Permission Request Template (Visual/Audio Asset)

> **This is a communication template, not a legally binding contract.** It is meant to gather the information needed to evaluate whether a specific asset can be marked `VERIFIED` under [`../docs/03-visual-asset-rights-policy.md`](../docs/03-visual-asset-rights-policy.md). Any resulting agreement should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before being relied upon, especially for commercial use at scale.
